What Maria is

Maria is an information service. She watches flight prices you ask about and tells you what she makes of them. She is not a travel agent, tour operator or booking site: she does not sell flights, take payment for travel, or hold your money. When you decide to book, you do so directly with an airline or seller, under their terms.

No guarantees on prices

Fares change constantly and availability can disappear between Maria finding a price and you booking it. Prices shown are indicative and may be wrong or out of date. Always confirm the final price and conditions at the point of booking. Baggage allowances, fare rules and seller policies are summarised for convenience and may be incomplete.

Seller checks

Maria screens sellers against public signals such as ATOL and IATA listings and Trustpilot ratings, and will say when something looks risky. This is a best-effort filter to help you avoid obvious traps — it is not an endorsement, a guarantee, or financial protection. Whether a booking is ATOL protected is determined by the seller and what you buy, not by Maria.
