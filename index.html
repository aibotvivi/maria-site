What a price alert can't do

🗺️

She plans trips, not legs

Combines one-way fares and self-transfer routings into whole-trip totals that round-trip search engines miss.

🛡️

She checks the seller, not just the price

Screens cheap fares against ATOL/IATA accreditation and Trustpilot before recommending. She won't headline a scammy travel site.

She earns a commission on some bookings — and she'll still tell you when the cheap one's a scam. Screening happens before she knows who pays. If the honest answer is “book direct with the airline”, that's the answer you get.

🧾

She counts the real cost

Factors in baggage fees, so a "cheap" fare that charges £50 for a bag doesn't fool the comparison — flagged as an estimate when an airline hides its fees.

Not a faster search. A second pair of eyes.

FAQ

About Maria
Is Maria a real person?No — she's a bot, built in London by someone who got tired of watching friends lose money to fake booking sites. She writes like a person because forms are miserable, not because she's pretending to be one.
Does a human ever see my messages?Yes. Maria is run by one person, and your chats sit on her machine, so she can read them — that's how a beta gets fixed. They're never sold or shared, and the models that write Maria's replies don't train on them. Privacy.
What if she's wrong?She might be. Prices move fast and airlines hide fees. Always confirm at booking — she gives you the link and the reasoning, you make the call.
Using her
Is it free?Free during the beta, and free for good on a basic number of watches. If paid tiers ever arrive, beta users get told first and keep beta terms.
How do I get in?Leave your email. Requests are approved by hand, in small batches.
When does she reply?Usually within a minute or two. Overnight messages get answered in the morning.
What happens to my data?Your watches and chats are yours. Not sold, not shared. Privacy.
Ask Maria is an information service, not a travel agent or booking site — she doesn't sell travel or take payment for it. Prices change; always confirm at booking. Some booking links (e.g. Aviasales) are affiliate links: booking through them may earn Ask Maria a commission at no extra cost to you, and this never affects which fare she recommends.
