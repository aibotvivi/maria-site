What a price alert can't do

🗺️

She plans trips, not legs

Combines one-way fares and self-transfer routings into whole-trip totals that round-trip search engines miss.

🛡️

She checks the seller, not just the price

Screens cheap fares against ATOL/IATA accreditation and Trustpilot before recommending. She won't headline a scammy travel site.

🧾

She counts the real cost

Factors in baggage fees, so a "cheap" fare that charges £50 for a bag doesn't fool the comparison — flagged as an estimate when an airline hides its fees.
