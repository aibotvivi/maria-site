Three things a price alert doesn’t do.

She plans trips, not legs

Combines one-way fares and self-transfer routings into a whole-trip total, with rules about how long a gap you’ll accept between legs.

She remembers every price

Not “prices look low” — “£487, the lowest since May.” She has written down every check since the day she started watching your route.

She counts the real cost

Baggage fees included. In the beta she caught a one-stop fare advertised as cheaper that cost more than the nonstop once a bag was added.

And you set the number, not her. “Tell me if Tokyo goes under £600” watches for your price, not someone else’s idea of a good deal.

The bit search engines can’t do

A trip is rarely one round-trip fare.

Give her the legs and one total and she tracks the running cost of the whole thing, rather than alerting on each flight separately. Up to four trips and twenty routes at once.

    Questions people actually ask

    Is Maria a real person?

    No — she’s a bot, built in London by someone who got tired of watching friends lose money to fake booking sites. She writes like a person because forms are miserable, not because she’s pretending to be one.

    Is it free?

    Free during the beta, and free for good on a basic number of watches. If paid tiers ever arrive, beta users get told first and keep beta terms.

    How do I get in?

    Leave your email and she hands you the Telegram link straight away. Places are limited, so if she’s full you’ll wait a little — she’ll tell you where you are in the queue.

    When does she reply?

    Usually within a minute or two. Overnight messages get answered in the morning.

    Can she watch a whole trip, not just one flight?

    Yes. Give her the legs and one total — “Cusco to Lima to Cancún, end of March, under £300 for the lot” — and she tracks the running cost of the whole thing rather than alerting on each flight separately. Up to four trips and twenty routes at once.

    What if she’s wrong?

    She might be. Prices move fast and airlines hide fees. Always confirm at booking — she gives you the link and the reasoning, you make the call.

    Does a human ever see my messages?

    Yes. Maria is run by one person, and your chats sit on her machine, so she can read them — that’s how a beta gets fixed. They’re never sold or shared, and the models that write Maria’s replies don’t train on them.

    What happens to my data?

    Your watches and chats are yours. Not sold, not shared. Deleted on request.
